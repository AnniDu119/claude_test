# One-Day Meal Plan for Marathon Preparation

Here is a one-day meal plan for an athlete preparing for marathon competition with optimal energy for long-distance endurance training:

* **Calorie Target:** Approximately 3,100 calories
* **Macronutrient Breakdown:** Protein (115g), Fat (85g), Carbs (420g)

## Meal Plan:

* **Breakfast (7:00 AM):** Oatmeal (100g dry weight) with banana (120g), honey (20g), and almond butter (25g). Orange juice (240ml).
  * Protein: 12g, Fat: 10g, Carbs: 85g

* **Mid-Morning Snack (10:00 AM):** Energy bar (50g) and dates (60g).
  * Protein: 6g, Fat: 8g, Carbs: 55g

* **Lunch (1:00 PM):** Grilled turkey breast (140g) sandwich on whole wheat bread (80g) with lettuce (30g), tomato (40g), and olive oil spread (15g). Mixed fruit salad with berries (150g).
  * Protein: 38g, Fat: 15g, Carbs: 75g

* **Pre-Training Snack (3:30 PM):** White rice cakes (40g) with honey (15g) and coconut oil (10g). Sports drink (500ml).
  * Protein: 3g, Fat: 10g, Carbs: 70g

* **Dinner (7:00 PM):** Grilled chicken breast (150g) with sweet potato (200g), roasted asparagus (150g), and olive oil (15g).
  * Protein: 42g, Fat: 20g, Carbs: 65g

* **Evening Snack (9:30 PM):** Rice cakes (30g) with sunflower seed butter (20g) and sliced apple (100g).
  * Protein: 8g, Fat: 12g, Carbs: 45g

This meal plan prioritizes complex carbohydrates and lean proteins to fuel long-distance training, while avoiding all dairy products. The timing and composition support sustained energy, muscle recovery, and marathon endurance goals.
